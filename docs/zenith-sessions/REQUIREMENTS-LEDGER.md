## Quality-loop launch queue checkpoint (Z1251, R-launch-1)

**CAP-14-03, CAP-08-02.** Z1251 (`97c26d9`): a restart from a valid primary brings a request that was still Queued or Blocked back as a cancellable `Blocked`/`Interrupted` row that reserves nothing, shown as "Not started · interrupted" with Cancel as its only action. **CAP-08-06, PROD-CORE-03.** R-launch-1 (`037fc62`, wording refined by M-L2-1 `d6e3e83`) adds a refusal backoff. A refusal is the provider's own Rejected answer before any client started: `StartupRejected` with no process claim, or a provider-reported `PreparationRejected`. That answer counts whatever its cause, including the provider's own local faults and a private Roblox page the user closed or cancelled (N-B2-3). Faults the coordinator catches itself, timeouts, a stop from Zenith Sessions, expiry, cleanup failure, a changed account and uncertain outcomes are not counted, even though most of them also end before any client started. The Guide hedges the same way (O-C2-1): a timeout or stop during native startup is recorded `Uncertain` with `TimedOut` or `CancelledDuringStartup`, whose rows say a client may exist, so the Guide says only that such a start does not lengthen the wait and that a timed-out or stopped startup may still have left a client to reconcile. The pause notice and the backoff status use the same word (O-C2-2): they say the starts were refused before any client started, never that they ended before any client started, because a timed-out, expired or stopped start also ends that way and never counts, and after a Resume that phrase would promise a re-pause the queue does not make. The refused rows (`StartupRejected`, `PreparationRejected`) point to the request's native launch stages in Launch evidence as a "may", with the limits named, not as a promise (O-C2-3): those stages are memory-only `INativeLaunchDiagnosticSource` snapshots (this app run, at most 128), while the Failed row is saved and reopens after a restart with none to show, and `PreparationRejected` is also recorded by the coordinator itself for a fault it caught or an invalid prepared handle, where no provider stage names a cause. After *n* refusals in a row the next gap is min(spacing × 2^*n*, 60 s) plus jitter clamped to ±1 s, and never less than the spacing; zero spacing stays zero. The second refusal in a row pauses the queue with `LaunchQueuePauseReason.RepeatedRefusals` and one cause-neutral notice. The remaining rows stay Queued, and nothing is cancelled or advanced to a new stop generation. A started client ends the streak, and a refusal 10 minutes or more after the previous one counts as the first again. An explicit Resume clears the reason but keeps the streak, so one more refusal pauses again. A refusal that lands after Panic is not counted. The streak, the backoff gate and the pause reason are memory-only: there is no timer and no saved deadline, and `PauseReason` is not written, so queue format 7 is unchanged. Only the paused flag is saved, and a restart always opens paused with no reason. Refused requests are never retried automatically; the 0.56 batch retry stays an explicit review.
